About

Jayson Virissimo is a Staff Software Engineer at SUBSCRIBE, where he's helping build the operating system for the alternative investments industry. He works primarily in Ruby on Rails, with Postgres, React, and GraphQL across the stack. Outside of work he builds learning tools grounded in spaced repetition — most recently Altkata and LedgerLore, native apps for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. He lives in Arizona.
