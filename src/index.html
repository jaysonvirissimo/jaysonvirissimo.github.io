About

Jayson Virissimo is a Staff Software Engineer at SUBSCRIBE, where he owns partner integrations for a regulated alternative-investments platform: architecture, compliance rules, data pipelines, incident response, and the partner relationship. He works in Ruby on Rails with Postgres, GraphQL, and React, and likes the parts of financial software that have to be boringly correct: idempotent pipelines, locks and constraints, authenticated webhooks, and tests written before the code.

Before that he managed the checkout and payments team at Tuft & Needle and built messaging features at Handshake. Outside of work he builds learning tools grounded in spaced repetition, most recently Altkata and LedgerLore, native apps for iPhone, iPad, and Mac. He lives in Arizona.
